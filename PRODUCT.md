# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Georgian developers, municipalities, architecture bureaus, and NGOs who need
environmental documentation to clear a permit. They arrive under deadline
pressure, usually mid-project, often after discovering a study is mandatory.
Their context: a desktop browser during the workday, sometimes a phone on site.

The job to be done: decide within about ninety seconds whether GREENWISE is
credible enough to hand a permit-blocking study to, and find the fastest route
to a scoped quote.

## Product Purpose

A marketing site for an environmental consulting firm. It exists to convert
qualified inbound enquiries into WhatsApp conversations and consultation
requests. Success = the visitor understands which of the four services their
project needs, believes the firm is technically serious, and makes contact.

## Brand Personality

Credible, grounded, unhurried. Speaks like a senior field scientist explaining
a finding to a client who is not a scientist: plain, exact, no salesmanship.
Emotional goal is relief — "these people have done this before, they will not
make my permit late."

## Reference

greenwise.info, chosen by the client. Take from it: photo-led composition,
generous whitespace, a light neutral ground with green as the committed
accent, the paired mission/vision device, animated stat counters, a three-up
value grid, and rounded-photo section pairs. It is a different company
(European biomethane and green hydrogen) — its structure and UI language
transfer, its content does not.

## Anti-references

- The previous GREENWISE build: dark gradient hero, no photography, uniform
  four-up icon-card grid, an eyebrow above every section.
- Generic SaaS landing pages: hero-metric templates, gradient text, glass cards.
- Stock "eco" clichés: leaf-motif wallpaper, recycled-paper textures.

## Design Principles

1. **Evidence over adjectives.** Every claim carries a number, a deliverable,
   or a named method. Trust is built from specifics, not from the word "trusted."
2. **Photography does the emotional work.** Real field, forest, and survey
   imagery carries the credibility; type and color stay disciplined around it.
3. **Georgian first.** Noto Sans Georgian throughout, no faked uppercase, no
   letter-spacing tricks the script does not support. Latin is the guest.
4. **Every section answers a client question.** No section exists to fill
   vertical space; each maps to "what, who, how, proof, or how do I start."
5. **Permit-speed is the promise.** Timeframes, scope, and cost transparency
   are surfaced, not buried.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1, verified against final tokens.
Visible keyboard focus on every interactive element. All motion, including stat
counters and scroll reveals, has a `prefers-reduced-motion` path that renders
final state immediately. Content never gated behind a JS-triggered reveal.
