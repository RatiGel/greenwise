# English translation — terms needing your sign-off

**What this is:** The GREENWISE website now exists in English as well as Georgian.
Most of the English text is a straightforward translation and needs no review.
But a number of Georgian words are legal, regulatory, or technical terms that
do not have one single "correct" English equivalent — translating them was a
judgement call, not a lookup. This document lists every one of those calls in
one place so you, as the person who knows the regulatory and business context,
can check them before the English site is shown to a bank, donor, client, or
any other outside reader. You do not need to read the rest of the site for
this — just this table.

## A note on spelling

The English copy uses **British spelling** throughout — "programme," "prioritise,"
"organisations," "fulfilment," "licences," "rigour," and so on. This was a
deliberate convention decision for the whole site, not an inconsistency or a
mistake. If you would prefer American spelling instead, that is a global
find-and-replace, not a per-term fix — let the team know and it can be handled
in one pass.

## What was NOT changed

No capability claims, certifications, or numbers were invented or altered in
translation. Every figure quoted in the English site matches the Georgian
exactly: **13+ years** of experience, **240+** completed projects,
**60,000+** trees inventoried, **40+** recurring clients, and in the team
bios, **"over 15 years"** of experience and **"80+ expert studies."** This
was checked and independently verified during review — nothing in the English
version overstates or understates what the Georgian version says.
